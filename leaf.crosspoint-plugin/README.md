Leaf: Reading Tracker

Leaf is a reading tracker app for iPhone and iPad, with Android coming soon. This plugin connects your reader to it: when you leave a book or the reader goes to sleep, Leaf gets how long you read and how far you got, and moves the book's bookmark there. Your time, pages and streaks show up in Leaf's stats.

Connect once:
1. In Leaf: Settings > E-reader > Connect a reader. Leaf shows a code.
2. On the reader: File Transfer. Open the address it shows in a browser on the same Wi-Fi.
3. In the browser: Settings, the Leaf card. Type the code, press Connect.

The first time you read a book, Leaf asks which of your books it is. After that every session lands on its own.

Sessions are sent when the reader connects to Wi-Fi or goes to sleep. The reader keeps about a dozen sessions while offline, so connect to Wi-Fi now and then.
